    A little more clarity

    Before you order.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Add your list and delivery details, then send the prepared request in WhatsApp.

    When is my order confirmed?

    Opening WhatsApp does not confirm an order. The pharmacy first checks stock, prescription requirements and prices, then asks you to approve the quote.

    How much does delivery cost?

    The delivery provider quotes a fare based on the actual route and availability. The pharmacy shares that fare separately before booking. There is no live fare estimate on this page.

    What happens to my details?

    Your form stays in this page’s memory and is not saved to a customer database. Sending the request shares it through WhatsApp. The pharmacy uses your address and contact information to coordinate an approved delivery.
